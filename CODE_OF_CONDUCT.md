# Community Code of Conduct

RequestDock welcomes people of any background and experience level. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces maintained by the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, and user needs with respect.
- Make feedback specific and actionable; explain disagreement without attacking a person.
- Be patient with first-time contributors and language differences.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, privacy, and credit for their work.

Harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, sexualized unwanted attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Neither are spam, fake endorsements, purchased engagement, or repeated low-value promotional submissions.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report content** control on abusive content or a user's **Block or report** control when a private report to GitHub is appropriate. These are GitHub's reporting channels, not a private project-maintainer inbox. Do not publish another person's private details as part of a report.

For a non-sensitive project moderation concern, open an issue describing the behavior and link to public evidence. The project has not established a dedicated private conduct-reporting address. A verified, consenting contact should be added before expanding to additional community spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request a change in behavior, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. They should explain the applicable rule and reason when safe to do so. Severity, repetition, and risk to others inform the response; there is no promise of a staffed response time.

A participant may request review of a moderation decision through an existing issue, without repeating harmful content. There is currently no independent appeals panel; the repository owner makes the final project decision.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public conduct reports.

## 中文摘要

讨论围绕代码、证据与用户需求，尊重不同背景、语言与经验。禁止骚扰、歧视、威胁、公开他人隐私、刷量和垃圾推广。可使用 GitHub 的举报入口向平台私密举报；该入口不等于维护者邮箱。项目尚未建立专门的私密行为投诉联系方式。维护者可依违规严重程度移除内容或限制参与，并在安全可行时说明理由。
